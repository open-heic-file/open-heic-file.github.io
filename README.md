# Open HEIC File

A minimalist, free online tool to open and view HEIC / HEIF photos directly in your web browser.

## Try It Online

[Open HEIC File & HEIC Viewer Online](https://open-heic-file.github.io/)

## Features

- **Open iPhone Photos**: Instantly view `.heic` and `.heif` images taken on iOS devices.
- **Cross-Platform Support**: Works seamlessly on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, iOS, and Chromebook.
- **100% Private**: Your photos are processed entirely within your browser. No files are ever uploaded to any server.
- **Save as JPG**: Convert and download your HEIC pictures as standard JPEG images with one click.
- **Interactive Controls**: Zoom in/out, rotate, and pan across your photos effortlessly.
- **Light & Dark Mode**: Switch between clean light theme and sleek dark theme.

## How to Use

1. Open [Open HEIC File](https://open-heic-file.github.io/) in any web browser.
2. Drag and drop your `.heic` files into the drop area or click **Open HEIC File**.
3. Preview, rotate, zoom, or download your converted JPG photos.
